# Air Service (Uber Air) Take-Home Test

## Business context

Uber has launched **Uber Air**, a marketplace that matches aeroplane operators
with individuals and groups who need transport. The service wants to
understand:

- regional growth drivers,
- the customer segments it serves well,
- its use cases (distance, geography, price tier, group/seat size, aircraft
  type),
- portfolio-comparable metrics, including DAU/WAU/MAU and revenue.

Uber Air aims to facilitate 20% of global aeroplane rides by 2030.

## The request

Design and implement a reliable, scalable, maintainable, and user-friendly
analytical data model that supports both ongoing monitoring and self-service
analysis for the business areas above.

The analysis the model must support includes:

- **Regional growth** — where the business is growing and why.
- **Customer segments and groups** — which customers and customer groups the
  service serves well.
- **Use cases** — distance, geography, price tier, group/seat size, and
  aircraft type.
- **Engagement** — daily, weekly, and monthly active users.
- **Revenue** — portfolio-comparable revenue metrics.

## Deliverables

Deliver the implemented analytical model together with documentation
explaining its design.
